# Indian macro scenario analysis

Build conditional scenarios, not point forecasts.

1. Identify the event, announcement date/time, affected policy channel and what the market already expects.
2. Separate direct exposures (banks, NBFCs, exporters, importers, oil marketing, airlines, metals, IT, autos, agriculture) from second-order effects.
3. Construct bear/base/bull scenarios with explicit assumptions, plausible ranges and evidence for each.
4. Consider RBI rates/liquidity, fiscal policy, inflation, crude, USD/INR, US yields, FII flows, monsoon and domestic demand.
5. Identify the sectors and balance-sheet characteristics most exposed; distinguish short-term sentiment from earnings impact.
6. State what data would confirm or invalidate each scenario. Avoid invented probabilities and unsupported target prices.
