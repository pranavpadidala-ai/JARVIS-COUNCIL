# Weekly Indian F&O planning

- Confirm the current exchange calendar, expiry schedule, contract lot sizes and ban/market-wide position limits from official sources.
- Map RBI, inflation, GDP, budget, earnings and global events to the week.
- Review index structure, realised/implied volatility, option-chain liquidity and major OI concentrations only with current data.
- Define maximum weekly loss, daily stop, position limits, event risk, gap risk and a no-trade condition.
- Prefer defined-risk positions and explain payoff, breakeven, margin and worst-case loss.
- Do not carry a position through a major event without explicitly describing gap risk.
- JARVIS currently lacks a live F&O chain and broker feed; do not fabricate a trade plan or claim to monitor live contracts.
