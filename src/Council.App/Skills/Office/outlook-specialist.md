# Outlook expert

## Writing emails people answer
- Subject says the ask and deadline: "Review needed: Chapter 3 draft by Fri 28 Nov".
- First line = the request; then context in 2-4 short paragraphs or bullets; end with a clear next step. Attach files with clear names; mention attachments in the text.
- Tone for professors/guides: polite, concise, specific ("Could you review Section 3.2 on HAZ widths by Friday?"). Offer a draft the user can edit; JARVIS never sends email on its own.
- Use Reply All only when everyone needs the answer; move people to BCC when replying to large groups.

## Inbox organisation
- Focused Inbox on; a few folders (Action, Waiting, Reference, Archive) instead of many; Archive (Backspace in new Outlook) rather than delete.
- Rules (File > Manage Rules & Alerts, or Settings > Mail > Rules in new Outlook): e.g. move newsletters to a folder, flag mail from the guide.
- Quick Steps for repeated multi-step actions (move + mark read + reply with template).
- Flags and follow-up reminders; Search with operators: `from:guide@college.edu hasattachments:yes subject:thesis received:this week`.
- Templates: save frequent replies as My Templates (new Outlook) or Quick Parts.

## Calendar and meetings
- New Meeting: required vs optional attendees, Scheduling Assistant to find free time, Teams meeting toggle for an online link, time zone check for international meetings.
- Recurring meetings with an end date; reminders 15 minutes for local, 1 day for things needing preparation.
- Share calendar (Calendar > Share) with view-only rights; overlay calendars to compare.
- Automatic replies (File > Automatic Replies / Settings > Accounts > Automatic replies) with dates, a different message for outside the organisation.

## Housekeeping
- Signature: Settings > Mail > Compose and reply; name, role, institution, phone; keep it short.
- Mailbox full: sort by size, empty Deleted Items, use Online Archive; old desktop Outlook can export to .pst (File > Open & Export).
- Attachments too large: share a OneDrive/Google Drive link instead (JARVIS google_drive can upload and give the link).

## JARVIS boundaries
JARVIS drafts emails, meeting texts and rules in the chat for the user to paste. It does not send, delete or forward email by itself; any sending needs the user's explicit OK each time.
