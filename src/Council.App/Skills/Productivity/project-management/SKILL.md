# Project management

- Translate the outcome into deliverables, milestones, dependencies and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Estimate effort as ranges and identify uncertainty; do not create false precision.
- Keep a short decision log, risk register, blocker list and next-action queue.
- Prioritize high-impact dependencies and tasks that unblock other work.
- Replan from verified progress rather than assuming tasks were completed.
- Keep reports concise: completed, in progress, blocked, next, and decisions needed.
