# Meeting intelligence

- Preserve the original recording. Create a transcript with timestamps where possible.
- Detect language and code-switching; do not translate unless requested. Retain technical names and acronyms, with uncertain words marked.
- Separate speakers only when diarization is reliable. Do not attribute statements to the user without evidence.
- Produce concise summary, decisions, unresolved questions, owners and deadlines. Mark unknown owners/dates rather than inventing them.
- Ask for review of uncertain transcript segments before using them to infer a personal language profile.
- Keep private recordings local by default. Obtain appropriate consent before processing or sharing other participants' speech.
