# Research paper writing

- Identify target venue, audience, word limits, formatting rules and contribution before drafting.
- Build the argument from evidence: problem, gap, objective, method, validation, results, interpretation and limitations.
- Ensure each claim is supported by data, a verified citation or an explicitly stated assumption.
- Do not overstate novelty, generality or causal conclusions.
- Keep terminology, symbols, units, figure references and citation formatting consistent.
- Preserve the author's scientific meaning during language editing and flag missing evidence instead of inventing it.
